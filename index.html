动态因子模型 (DFM)

Dynamic Factor Model

DFM是一种统计降维技术，从16个高维宏观指标中提取出10个低维核心因子，保留原始数据的主要信息和波动特征。

DFM is a statistical dimensionality reduction technique that extracts 10 low-dimensional core factors from 16 high-dimensional macro indicators, preserving the main information and volatility characteristics of the original data.

六层技术框架

Six-Layer Technical Framework

DFM 动态因子模型 Dynamic Factor Model
从多变量序列中提取核心因子，捕捉市场深层驱动
Extracts core factors from multivariate sequences, capturing deep market drivers
PCMCI 因果发现 Causal Discovery
识别因子间的因果路径和滞后效应，过滤虚假相关性
Identifies causal paths and lag effects between factors, filters spurious correlations
HMM 隐马尔可夫链状态检测 Hidden Markov Model
识别低/中/高波动市场状态，实现动态风控切换
Identifies low/medium/high volatility market regimes for dynamic risk control switching
Mamba-MoE 核心预测 Core Prediction
专家混合架构，给每个专家最合适的权重进行预测
Mixture of Experts architecture, assigns optimal weights to each expert for prediction
SHAP 因子归因 Factor Attribution
解释模型决策，生成可追溯的归因报告
Explains model decisions, generates traceable attribution reports
基于状态的风控策略 Regime-Based Risk Control
根据市场波动状态动态调整对冲策略，降低风险敞口
Dynamically adjusts hedging strategy based on market volatility regime to reduce exposure
